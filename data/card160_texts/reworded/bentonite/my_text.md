Compared to other clay minerals like illite, smectite, kaolinite, and halloysite, weathered biotite shows superior cesium sorption, often by two orders of magnitude . [[a973183290]]

Montmorillonite also has a high adsorption capacity, performing better than illite and kaolinite . [[ec9d65184a]] [[451becad04]]

Specific bentonite types containing montmorillonite are effective: Kunipia-F bentonite outperforms SiO2 , natural Ca-bentonite has a greater capacity than Na-bentonite , and inorganic modified Na-bentonite adsorbs strongly under alkaline conditions . [[1cc79421db]] [[91a1c20b01]] [[0adc4e6c6d]]

Cesium removal is also enhanced by molybdenum vanadate loaded bentonite . [[8d56fcd23b]]

Glauconite shows greater sorption than bentonite , and acid activation of kaolinite significantly improves natural kaolinite's efficiency . [[32d6151f27]] [[7e3a1a3ed3]]

While zeolite has the highest overall sorption of all minerals , weathered biotite appears to be the best clay material for sorption. [[32d6151f27]]
